Chapter XXXIII

Phrases

Six kinds of Phrases

(1) Noun Phrase — does the work of a Noun.
যে Phrase Noun-এর কাজ করে।
  • To ride well requires practice.
  • He likes to play tennis.
(2) Adjective Phrase — does the work of an Adjective.
Noun/Pronoun-কে বিশেষিত করে।
  • He gave me a chain of gold.
  • The man in the street knows it.
(3) Adverbial Phrase — does the work of an Adverb.
ক্রিয়াকে বিশেষিত করে।
  • He worked with care.
  • This must be done at any price.
(4) Prepositional Phrase — does the work of a Preposition.
  • He stood in front of me.
  • He could not come on account of illness.
(5) Conjunctional Phrase · (6) Interjectional Phrase
Chapter XXXIV

Clauses

What is a Clause?

A Clause is a sentence forming part of a bigger sentence.
বৃহত্তর বাক্যের অংশ; নিজস্ব Subject ও Finite Verb আছে।

Remember: number of clauses = number of finite verbs.

Kinds of Clauses

Principal — complete sense alone.
Subordinate — depends on another clause.
Co-ordinate — same rank, joined by and/but/or.

A. Noun Clause

Does the work of a Noun.
Subject, Object, Complement বা Apposition।
  • I know that he is ill.
  • That he is ill is known to all.

B. Adjective Clause

Qualifies a noun or pronoun.
Noun/Pronoun-কে বিশেষিত করে।
  • I know the boy who did it.
  • This is the place where he was born.
Restrictive = defines. Continuative = extra detail (= and he/it).

C. Adverbial Clause

Does the work of an Adverb — Time, Place, Reason, Purpose, Result, Manner, Condition, Concession, Comparison.
  • He came when I was there.
  • Stay where you are.
  • Though he is poor, he is honest.

Function decides the kind

  • I know where he lives — Noun
  • I know the place where he lives — Adjective
  • I shall go where he lives — Adverbial
Chapter XXXV

Simple, Complex, Compound

Simple

One subject and one finite verb.
একটি Subject ও একটি Finite Verb।

Complex

One Principal + one or more Subordinate clauses.
একটি Principal + Subordinate Clause(s)।

Compound

Two or more Principal clauses joined by co-ordinating conjunctions.
দুই বা ততোধিক Principal Clause।
Chapters XXXVI–XXXVII

Analysis of Sentences

Simple Sentence analysis

  1. Find Finite Verb
  2. Find Subject
  3. Note Adjuncts
  4. Note Object / Complement / Adverbial Adjuncts

Complex analysis

  1. Divide by finite verbs
  2. Find Principal clause
  3. Classify subordinates (Noun / Adjective / Adverbial)

Compound analysis

  1. Identify principal co-ordinates
  2. Identify any subordinates
Chapter XXXVIII

Difficulties in Analysis

Quoted Sentences

A quotation is not a Noun Clause — treat as noun-equivalent object. He said, "I am ill" is Simple.

Parenthetic Expressions

I believe in "who, I believe, did it" is Parenthetic, not Principal/Subordinate.

Ellipses

Supply understood words: He is stronger than I → than I (am strong).

Chapter XXXIX

Syntax

Subject–Verb Agreement

A verb must agree with its subject in number and person.
Verb-কে Subject-এর Number ও Person অনুযায়ী মিলতে হবে।
